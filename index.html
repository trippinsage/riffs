Our Story Since 1939

For over 85 years, Riff's has been part of the fabric of Newfoundland & Labrador life. Family-owned, community-first, and proudly local.

1939: The Beginning

A small general store opened in Windsor, Newfoundland and began a legacy built on trust and value.

1970s to 1990s: Growth

As communities grew, Riff's opened new stores from coast to coast across the province.

Today: 20 Stores Strong

Still 100% family-owned. Still committed to quality, value, and the people of NL.
